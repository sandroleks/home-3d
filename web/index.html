All controls

Moving

  • W A S D walk
  • Shift walk faster
  • Mouse look around
  • C sit on the floor, or get up (moving gets you up too)
  • Esc pause

Using things

  • Click open doors, flip light switches, sit on sofas and chairs, lie on the bed
  • L toggle this room's light
  • Click a chair at the chessboard, the backgammon case or the Go board on the dining table to play; C stands up
  • T the TV: its remote, and draw and guess with the others (or click the TV)

Time and light

  • , . change the time
  • Shift + , . change the date
  • P play the day
  • R real sun and sky on or off
  • K kitchen wall up (as it is now) or down
  • Tab free the mouse for the sun panel, keep walking

Sound and voice

  • M mute sound
  • V mic on or off
  • G who's here
